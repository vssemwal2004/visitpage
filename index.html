FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question?

Send an enquiry and an admission counsellor will get in touch with you.

Ask a counsellor
Is Graphic Era (Deemed to be University) recognised?

Yes. The university is approved by UGC and has Regularized Status from the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It is NAAC A+ accredited (2022), AICTE approved, and ranked #48 among universities in NIRF 2025.

Which programmes can I apply for?

140+ programmes across 21 departments: undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate and executive. See the full list in the Programmes section.

How do I apply for 2027–28?

Fill the enquiry form and a counsellor will guide you. Enrolment takes 6 simple steps: choose your programme, start the application, upload documents, review, pay the application fee and confirm your seat. See Admissions.

What is the application fee?

₹1,500 for all courses, paid online by UPI, card or net banking.

What is GECET?

The Graphic Era Common Entrance Test is an all-India scholarship test by the Graphic Era Group of Institutions. Depending on your score, you can get up to a 100% tuition fee waiver.

Are other scholarships available?

Yes. Merit-based scholarships, plus additional support for female candidates and for wards of defence personnel. The admissions team will confirm your eligibility for 2027–28.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Yes, the Dehradun campus has on-campus hostels. Ask your counsellor about room options and fees.
